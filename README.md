## Brand Guidelines

These guidelines are recommendations for the proper representation of the DiceBear brand. They are independent of the license.

### Do

- Do use the DiceBear logo to link to [dicebear.com](https://www.dicebear.com)
- Do keep the original aspect ratio when resizing the logo
- Do use the black or `#0284C7` version of the logo on light backgrounds
- Do use a white version of the logo on dark backgrounds
- Do give the logo enough spacing so it remains clearly visible

### Don't

- Don't place the logo on noisy or low-contrast backgrounds
- Don't detach or rearrange the icon and wordmark
- Don't add additional colors or elements to the logo
- Don't use the logo to imply endorsement without permission
